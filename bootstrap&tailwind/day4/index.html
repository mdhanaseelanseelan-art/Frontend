RESUME BUILDING

A standard professional resume format includes five core sections in order: a contact header, a short professional summary, work experience in reverse chronological order, education, and key skills. Keep the layout clean, use 10 to 12 point font size for body text, limit length to one or two pages, and save it as a PDF.

SKILL DEVELOPMENT

The skill development process is a structured journey of identifying, acquiring, practicing, and refining a capability. It moves from an initial assessment of current abilities to targeted learning, real-world application, and ongoing progress measurement.Find your current skill level through self-evaluation, tests, or feedback.

INTERVIEW PREPARATION

To prepare for an interview, study the job description and research the company's mission and culture. Practice common questions using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), organize your resume copies, and plan professional attire and punctuality.

HR interview

Tell me about yourself: Keep your summary under two minutes, focusing on past work and future goals. your personal values to the company's mission.Strengths and weaknesses: Pick real job-related strengths with proof, and frame weaknesses as areas you actively work to improve.
